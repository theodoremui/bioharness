### Learning from a teacher with privileged information {#bg-teacher}

Privileged information can also be used to produce better demonstrations for supervised fine-tuning. A **teacher** model that receives the privileged information can often solve a task that a **student** without it cannot, and the student is then fine-tuned to imitate the teacher's trajectories. This is a form of **distillation**: knowledge moves from one model into another by imitation. It carries a known hazard. If the teacher's choices depend on information the student never receives, the student is trained to make moves it cannot justify from its own input, and it learns to imitate the moves without being able to repeat their success. @{penaloza2026} study this gap for language-model agents and propose training methods that narrow it ({fig:pidistill}).
