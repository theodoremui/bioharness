## Other changes made in this pass

- The best no-glyph renderer for H4 and E5, and every other choice of a best renderer, is made on dev-select people, so the sealed set is never used for selection (H4, l.169, l.219).
- For people, the forecasts and $A$'s gain are computed on the same pairwise judgments as the people's gains, so H1 compares like with like (Metrics).
- Prose trimmed from an audit count of 6,875 to 6,297 by removing repeated statements (risks already covered in Method, captions that repeated legends, an outcome row that duplicated another) and by moving dense inline definitions into display equations.
