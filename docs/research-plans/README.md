# Research plans: proposals 05 to 08

These comprehensive plans study self-improving measurement agents. Each develops its hypotheses, methods, experimental controls, statistical analysis, resource estimates and limitations. Proposal numbers continue from the earlier idea summaries.

| Proposal | Research question |
|---|---|
| [05. What makes a harness improvable](05-what-makes-a-harness-improvable.md) | What makes an agent harness easier to improve? |
| [06. Who self-improvement leaves behind](06-who-self-improvement-leaves-behind.md) | Who does self-improvement leave behind? |
| [07. Learning how to display tool outputs](07-learning-how-to-look-at-tool-outputs.md) | Can a perception test predict who else can read an agent's plots? |
| [08. Blind analysis for agents](08-blind-analysis-for-agents.md) | Can a clinician undo what the referral did to an agent's measurement? |

The [review record](review-record-05-08.md) documents how these plans were developed and revised. Their figures are in `images/05/` through `images/08/`, with the collection diagram in `images/overview/`. Figure-generation scripts, simulations and archived reviews remain in [output/idea-summaries-build](../../output/idea-summaries-build/).

See the [research overview](../research-overview.md) for all eight proposals, the [idea summaries](../idea-summaries/README.md) for proposals 01 to 04, and the [quality assessment](../quality-assessment.md) for a comparison across both collections.
